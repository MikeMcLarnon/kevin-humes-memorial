In honor of Kevin’s legacy, the Kevin Humes Memorial Foundation has proudly donated $500 to the Vetri Community Partnership. 

This impactful nonprofit organization brings nutrition education to life through the art of cooking, reaching more than 30,000 children and families annually across Philadelphia and Camden. Our contribution will help fund nutritious ingredients for a 10-week Vetri Cooking Lab workshop, empowering students to build healthy habits in the kitchen and beyond.☀️
