For the 6th year in a row, family and friends gathered to honor Kevin’s life, reflect on the love he shared, and remember the bright, kind, and unforgettable person he was.   

 

The Kevin Humes Memorial Foundation is grateful to everyone who continues to show up, give back, and help keep Kevin’s light shining. Each year, this event reminds us that although Kevin may no longer be with us, the love he shared continues to bring people together and inspire us to make a difference in our community.
