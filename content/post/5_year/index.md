May 16, 2025 marks five years since Kevin was taken from us, and not a day passes without his absence being deeply felt. As we reflect on his life, his laughter, and the countless ways he made the world brighter, we are reminded of the importance of carrying his legacy forward. 

In Kevin’s honor, the Kevin Humes Memorial Foundation has made a $1,000 donation to the [National Eagle Scout Association Scholarship Fund](https://give.scouting.org/a/nesa-legacy-society). Kevin was a proud Eagle Scout, and this contribution supports aspiring young leaders who exemplify the values, integrity, and spirit that he lived by every day. 

We invite you to take a moment to remember Kevin. And if you’re able, consider doing something kind — big or small — in his memory. ❤️
