We came together once again for the 5th Annual Memorial Walk in loving memory of Kevin. Each year, this gathering becomes more than just a walk—it becomes a powerful reminder of Kevin’s lasting impact on all of us. 

Under a beautiful sky and surrounded by familiar faces, friends, family, and supporters joined together to walk the 5K route with purpose and heart. Matching shirts, laughter, and heartfelt stories filled the day, as we reflected on Kevin’s legacy and the values he held dear—kindness, connection, and community. 

What began as a way to honor his memory has grown into a tradition that brings us closer, year after year. Thank you to everyone who participated, volunteered, and helped keep Kevin’s spirit alive. Your continued support means everything. 

We walk not just in remembrance, but in celebration of a life that continues to inspire.
