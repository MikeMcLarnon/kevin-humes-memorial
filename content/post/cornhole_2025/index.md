We are proud to share that the 5th Annual Kevin Humes Memorial Cornhole Tournament was a tremendous success, raising $6,000 to support the CHOP Holiday Toy Drive and other local charities that reflect the values Kevin held dear. 

This annual event has become a meaningful tradition — a day filled with friendly competition, community spirit, and heartfelt remembrance. From our generous donors to every participant, volunteer, and supporter, thank you for helping us honor Kevin’s legacy in such a powerful way. 

A special congratulations goes out to Jay and Joey for claiming the championship title this year! 

Together, we’re turning Kevin’s memory into action, creating real impact in the community he loved. We look forward to sharing more about how these funds will be used and the upcoming initiatives we have planned in Kevin’s memory.
